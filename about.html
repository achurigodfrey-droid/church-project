Our History

A Parish Born from Faith

Riabore Catholic Church was founded in the heart of Kisii County, Kenya, as a beacon of faith and service to the local community. Over the decades, the parish has grown from a small mission chapel into a thriving community of thousands of faithful Catholics.

Under the spiritual care of the Diocese of Kisii, Riabore has been a center of evangelization, education, social welfare, and worship. From the early missionaries who planted the seed of faith to the parishioners who nurture it today — the story of Riabore is a story of God's grace at work.

We are committed to ensuring that everyone, whether present physically or joining us remotely from across the world, feels at home in our parish family.

Mission & Vision

Guided by the Gospel of Jesus Christ

Our Mission

To proclaim the Good News of Jesus Christ, celebrate the sacraments, and serve the people of God with love, justice, and compassion — in person and through digital outreach.

Our Vision

A dynamic, welcoming Catholic community where every person encounters the living God, grows in discipleship, and reaches out to others in service and love.

Our Values

Faith, Community, Service, Dignity, Inclusivity, and the Eucharist as the source and summit of Christian life.
